# Aviator Prompts

**38 reusable AI prompts for general aviation study, debriefs, and aircraft ownership.**

Built by [Lloyd Clark](https://github.com/FlightDeckAI), commercial pilot, CFI, and founder of [Federal.AI](https://federal.ai/).

[Browse all prompts](prompts/INDEX.md) · [Start with training](prompts/training.md) · [Contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md)

**Status:** Educational prompt library. **License:** [MIT](LICENSE). No installation required.

> Use these prompts for preparation, study, and organizing information. AI outputs require verification against current authoritative references and instructor or mechanic review where appropriate. They do not replace a weather briefing, aircraft checklist, flight instruction, or an operational decision.

## Start in one minute

1. Choose a category below and copy a prompt.
2. Replace the bracketed fields with your own non-sensitive information.
3. Paste it into your preferred language model.
4. Review its assumptions and verify factual claims before using the result.

**A good first exercise:** open [Training](prompts/training.md) and use the lesson-debrief prompt to turn your own flight notes into three wins, three areas to improve, and a next-lesson objective.

## What a useful result looks like

A good answer should identify missing inputs, state assumptions, distinguish supplied facts from suggestions, and leave you with specific items to verify. Ask the model to say when it does not know.

Prompts that mention a CFI, DPE, or A&P use role-play to structure the response; the model does not hold those credentials.
